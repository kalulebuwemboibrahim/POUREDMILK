CLASSICS / INTERMEDIATE

Classic sponge
made to be understood.

A light sponge where eggs and air do most of the lifting.

45 minutes8 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 large eggs
  • 120 g caster sugar
  • 120 g plain flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 30 g melted butter
  • pinch fine salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C / 356°F. Line a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Whisk eggs and sugar over gentle heat until warm, then beat until thick, pale and ribbon-like.
  3. Sift the flour and salt over the foam and fold gently in two or three additions.
  4. Fold through the melted butter without knocking out more air than necessary.
  5. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and springy. Cool upside down if your tin and method allow it.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
