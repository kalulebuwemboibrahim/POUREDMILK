    03 / OVEN TEMPERATURE

    Read the heat.

    160°C / 325°F — gentle cakes and longer bakes
    175°C / 350°F — common cake temperature
    180°C / 356°F — moderate conventional oven
    190°C / 375°F — hotter, faster baking

    Fan ovens often need a reduction of about 15–20°C / 25–35°F. Your oven may vary, so use visual doneness cues too.

    04 / CAKE SERVINGS

    Estimate before you bake.

    20 cm round — 8–12 modest servings
    23 cm round — 12–16 modest servings
    20 × 30 cm rectangle — 15–24 modest servings
    25 cm round — 16–22 modest servings

    Portions depend on cake height, occasion and slice size. Treat these as planning numbers, not rules.
