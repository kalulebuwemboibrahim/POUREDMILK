Troubleshooting

Dense crumb, sinking centre, dry edges, greasy batter, uneven browning and broken frosting all leave clues. Learn to diagnose the likely cause before changing everything at once.

03 / DEEP DIVE

Diagnose before replacing

A sunken centre may point to excess liquid, weak structure, underbaking or opening the oven too early. Dry edges can indicate overbaking, a dark pan, thin batter or poor heat distribution. A greasy split batter can suggest an emulsion problem.

Write down what happened. Temperature, pan, mixing time and ingredient temperature are useful evidence. Troubleshooting becomes easier when you change one variable at a time.

POUREDMILK principle

Don't only memorise the instruction. Understand the reason behind it, then use that understanding when the situation changes.

What to look for

  • Identify the purpose of each major step.
  • Separate reliable principles from personal preference.
  • Observe the batter, crumb, heat and timing instead of relying on one number.
  • Record useful changes so your next bake becomes easier to predict.
