HISTORY

Cake has a
long memory.

CURRENT VERSION · 2004–2026

The modern cake is the result of a long conversation between bread, sweeteners, eggs, dairy, milling, ovens and celebration.

ANCIENT WORLD

Sweetened breads

Early cakes were often closer to enriched breads: grains mixed with sweeteners, dried fruit, nuts or dairy. The boundary between bread and cake was not fixed.

MEDIEVAL EUROPE

Spice and preservation

Honey, dried fruits and spices became important ways to add sweetness and flavour. Cakes remained closely tied to feasting and special occasions.

17TH–18TH C.

Better sugar and better ovens

Refined sugar became more available in Europe, while improved ovens and milling supported lighter, more delicate preparations.

19TH C.

Chemical leavening

Baking soda and baking powder changed what home bakers could achieve, making reliable lift possible without long fermentation or elaborate egg foams.

20TH C.

The home-baking era

Electric mixers, standardized tins, packaged ingredients and cookbooks made cake increasingly accessible and repeatable in ordinary kitchens.

TODAY

Everything at once

Modern cake culture moves freely between heritage recipes, professional pastry, supermarket convenience, social-media trends and experimental baking.
