Carrot cake
made to be understood.

Warm spice, grated carrot and a sturdy crumb built for frosting.

75 minutes10 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 240 g plain flour
  • 180 g brown sugar
  • 180 ml neutral oil
  • 3 large eggs
  • 250 g finely grated carrot
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ginger
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • pinch salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F. Line a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Whisk oil, sugar and eggs until smooth.
  3. Fold in flour, spices, leaveners and salt, followed by the grated carrot.
  4. Bake 45–50 minutes until a skewer comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  5. Cool fully before frosting; a warm carrot cake will soften the icing.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
