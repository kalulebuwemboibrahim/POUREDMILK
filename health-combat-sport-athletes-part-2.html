PART 02

Food, Timing & Portions
Look at regular meals, timing, portions, hydration and the practical choices that make occasional treats easier to enjoy.

Timing without obsession

For combat sports, timing should be practical rather than rigid. A treat can sit after a meal, during a social moment or at another comfortable time. The important part is that cake does not push out the foods and fluids that the routine depends on.

Portion thinking

  • Start with a portion you expect to enjoy.
  • Eat slowly enough to notice satisfaction.
  • If the cake is very rich, a smaller portion may be enough.
  • There is no universal “perfect” slice size.

Keep the bigger picture

Healthy Cake is educational, not a prescription. Individual energy needs, medical conditions, allergies, eating concerns and training demands can change what is appropriate. When a situation is personal or high-stakes, use qualified professional advice.

WHAT TO AVOID

All-or-nothing thinking

Do not turn cake into a moral test. One food does not decide whether a lifestyle is healthy.
