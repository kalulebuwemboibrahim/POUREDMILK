ABOUT POUREDMILK

A place for people
who love knowing why.

POUREDMILK is an independent information centre about cake: how it is made, where it came from, why it behaves the way it does, and how to make better sense of it.

Start with a recipeStart learning

THE IDEA

“A recipe tells you what to do. Understanding tells you what to do next.”

The site sits between a recipe book, a food magazine and a practical baking reference. It should feel useful when you are cooking, but interesting enough to browse when you are not.

01 / CLARITY

Explain the why.

We prefer useful explanations over unexplained rules. When a step matters, tell the baker what it is doing.

02 / CURIOSITY

Follow the question.

Good baking knowledge should lead somewhere: into history, chemistry, technique, culture or another recipe.

03 / PRACTICE

Make it usable.

Knowledge only helps when it survives contact with a mixing bowl, an oven and an actual Tuesday evening.

THE PERSON BEHIND POUREDMILK

Why I built this centre

I want POUREDMILK to grow beyond being another recipe website and become a major reference centre for cake around the world — a place to learn, explore, understand, bake and appreciate cake.

I also want to explore the health side of cake: how people can enjoy what they love while paying attention to lifestyle, activity and overall well-being.

And, just so you know a little more about me: I drink more healthily than I eat.
