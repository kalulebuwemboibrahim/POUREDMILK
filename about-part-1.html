01 / DEEP DIVE

A centre, not just a recipe shelf

POUREDMILK is designed as an information centre for cake. Recipes are one entrance; science, history, culture, ingredients, health and tools are other entrances to the same subject.

The goal is to let a curious visitor move from “how do I make this?” to “why does this work?” and then to “where did this idea come from?”

POUREDMILK principle

Don't only memorise the instruction. Understand the reason behind it, then use that understanding when the situation changes.

What to look for

  • Identify the purpose of each major step.
  • Separate reliable principles from personal preference.
  • Observe the batter, crumb, heat and timing instead of relying on one number.
  • Record useful changes so your next bake becomes easier to predict.
