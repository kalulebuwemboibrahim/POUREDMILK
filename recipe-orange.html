FRUIT / BEGINNER

Olive oil orange cake
made to be understood.

Citrus and olive oil make this cake aromatic and naturally tender.

60 minutes8 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 220 g plain flour
  • 170 g caster sugar
  • 150 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 oranges, zest and juice
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F and line a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Whisk eggs, sugar and orange zest until slightly thickened.
  3. Slowly whisk in the olive oil and orange juice.
  4. Fold in flour, baking powder and salt.
  5. Bake 40–45 minutes until golden and springy.
  6. Rest 15 minutes before turning out.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
