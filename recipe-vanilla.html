FOUNDATIONS / BEGINNER

The vanilla cake worth learning
made to be understood.

A dependable butter cake for learning the creaming method, structure and moisture.

55 minutes8 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 250 g plain flour
  • 200 g caster sugar
  • 170 g soft unsalted butter
  • 3 large eggs
  • 120 ml milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp fine salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F. Grease and line a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until noticeably lighter and fluffy. This is where you build useful air into the batter.
  3. Add the eggs gradually, mixing well after each addition. Stir in the vanilla.
  4. Fold in the flour, baking powder and salt in alternating additions with the milk. Stop as soon as the batter is even.
  5. Bake for 35–40 minutes, until the centre springs back and a skewer comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  6. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
