FRUIT / BEGINNER

Apple tea cake
made to be understood.

Thin apple slices, cinnamon and a simple batter.

65 minutes10 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 220 g plain flour
  • 160 g caster sugar
  • 120 g soft butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 120 ml milk
  • 2 apples
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • pinch salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F and line a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs.
  3. Fold in dry ingredients with milk.
  4. Slice apples thinly and arrange over the batter. Dust lightly with cinnamon.
  5. Bake 40–45 minutes until the apples are tender and the centre is set.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
