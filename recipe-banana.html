FRUIT / BEGINNER

Banana cake
made to be understood.

A practical way to turn very ripe bananas into a soft, fragrant cake.

60 minutes9 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 220 g plain flour
  • 150 g brown sugar
  • 100 g soft butter
  • 2 very ripe bananas
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 60 ml milk
  • pinch salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F and line a 20 cm square tin.
  2. Mash the bananas until mostly smooth.
  3. Cream butter and sugar, then beat in the eggs and banana.
  4. Fold in the dry ingredients and milk just until combined.
  5. Bake 35–40 minutes until the centre is set and the top is deeply golden.
  6. Cool before cutting; the crumb becomes more stable as it cools.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
