Activity, Recovery & Lifestyle
Connect movement, training or daily activity with recovery, sleep, hydration and realistic expectations.

Activity is not a punishment

For gaming, movement has its own purpose: fitness, performance, mood, recreation or daily function. Do not create a rule that says cake must be “worked off.” Instead, keep activity and eating as separate parts of a sustainable lifestyle.

Recovery basics

  • Fluids before and after demanding activity.
  • Regular meals that support the day.
  • Adequate sleep and rest.
  • More food when training demands genuinely rise.

Keep the bigger picture

Healthy Cake is educational, not a prescription. Individual energy needs, medical conditions, allergies, eating concerns and training demands can change what is appropriate. When a situation is personal or high-stakes, use qualified professional advice.

WHAT TO AVOID

All-or-nothing thinking

Do not turn cake into a moral test. One food does not decide whether a lifestyle is healthy.
