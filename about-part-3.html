ABOUT AUTHOR

KALULE IBRAHIM BUWEMBO
The person behind POUREDMILK

A personal introduction to the founder and author behind POUREDMILK.

MY STORY

Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh.

I am a Ugandan native. Outside my everyday responsibilities, I involve myself in leisure activities such as football, skating, and gaming. I am also a model by stature, something that has been part of my childhood dream.

But there is another thing about me that may not immediately come to mind:

I know a thing or two about cakes.

Like many other people, life eventually gets you focused on the things that put you at rest and help you move forward. I am an architect by profession, and at some point you have to start thinking seriously about what you want your future to look like and work towards building it.

I don't think a project like this is something people would normally expect from an architect, considering the direction of the profession. But I never hesitated to keep my knowledge and love for cakes with me.

My interest in cake has been there since childhood, and it has remained with me to this day. I became interested in cooking and baking through the trends and creativity surrounding food, but eventually I discovered that baking was the part I enjoyed the most.

That interest eventually became POUREDMILK.

I want POUREDMILK to grow beyond being just another recipe website. My ambition is for it to become a major reference centre for cake around the world — a place where people can come to learn, explore, understand, bake, and simply appreciate cake.

And while I love cake, I also care about how we live around the things we enjoy. I want to explore the health side of cake too — how people can enjoy their preferred treats while still paying attention to their lifestyle, activity, and overall well-being.

You don't necessarily have to give up something you love in order to take care of yourself.

And, just so you know a little more about me:

I drink more healthily than I eat.

Welcome to POUREDMILK — a project built from a genuine love for cake, curiosity about baking, and a desire to share that knowledge with the world.

Recipes. Stories. Science. History. Cake.
