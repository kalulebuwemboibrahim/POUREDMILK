RECIPE · CHOCOLATE · BEGINNER

Deep, simple
chocolate cake.

A dark, moist everyday cake designed to be forgiving. The method uses oil for tenderness and hot liquid to help bloom the cocoa.

Why it works

Cocoa contains flavour compounds that become more expressive when hydrated with hot liquid. Oil stays liquid when the cake cools, so the crumb remains soft. The batter is intentionally loose; that is part of the design.

Method

01 · PREPARE

Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F. Grease and line an 8-inch / 20 cm round cake tin.

02 · MIX DRY

Whisk flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda and salt until evenly distributed.

03 · MIX WET

Whisk eggs, milk, oil and vanilla separately. Pour into the dry ingredients and mix only until combined.

04 · BLOOM

Slowly add hot coffee or hot water and whisk until the batter is smooth. It will be thin.

05 · BAKE

Pour into the tin and bake for roughly 30–38 minutes, until the centre springs back lightly and a tester comes out with a few moist crumbs.

06 · COOL

Rest in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out and cool completely before slicing.

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 200 g sugar
  • 45 g unsweetened cocoa
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 180 ml milk
  • 120 ml neutral oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 180 ml hot coffee or water
