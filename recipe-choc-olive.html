CHOCOLATE / BEGINNER

Chocolate olive oil cake
made to be understood.

Deep cocoa flavour with a soft, almost fudgy centre.

50 minutes8 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 180 g plain flour
  • 60 g cocoa powder
  • 180 g sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 120 ml olive oil
  • 180 ml milk
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • pinch salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F and line a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Whisk eggs, sugar, olive oil and vanilla.
  3. Sift in flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt. Add milk and whisk just until smooth.
  4. Bake 30–35 minutes; do not overbake if you want a fudgy centre.
  5. Cool in the tin before slicing.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
