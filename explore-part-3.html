03 / DEEP DIVE

Ingredient intelligence

Ingredients are functional materials. Flour contributes starch and protein. Eggs provide water, protein, fat and emulsifying power. Sugar changes sweetness, moisture and browning. Fat changes tenderness and carries aroma. Leaveners create gas. Salt sharpens flavour.

When adapting a recipe, change one major variable at a time. Think in terms of what the ingredient was doing, not only what it tasted like.

POUREDMILK principle

Don't only memorise the instruction. Understand the reason behind it, then use that understanding when the situation changes.

What to look for

  • Identify the purpose of each major step.
  • Separate reliable principles from personal preference.
  • Observe the batter, crumb, heat and timing instead of relying on one number.
  • Record useful changes so your next bake becomes easier to predict.
