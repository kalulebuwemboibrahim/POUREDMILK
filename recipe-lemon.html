FRUIT / BEGINNER

Lemon drizzle loaf
made to be understood.

Bright lemon, a tender crumb and a crisp syrup finish.

65 minutes10 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 175 g caster sugar
  • 150 g soft butter
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 lemons, zest and juice
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 50 ml milk
  • pinch salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F. Line a 900 g loaf tin.
  2. Cream butter and sugar until pale. Beat in eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in flour, baking powder and salt with the milk and most of the lemon zest.
  4. Bake 45–50 minutes until risen and golden.
  5. Mix the remaining lemon juice with 50 g sugar. Pour over the hot cake slowly so it soaks through the crust.
  6. Cool completely before slicing.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
