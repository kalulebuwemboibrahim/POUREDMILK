CELEBRATION / INTERMEDIATE

Coconut cake
made to be understood.

Soft coconut crumb with a lightly toasted finish.

70 minutes10 servings175°C / 350°F

INGREDIENTS

  • 220 g plain flour
  • 180 g caster sugar
  • 160 g soft butter
  • 3 large eggs
  • 100 g desiccated coconut
  • 120 ml coconut milk
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
Scale this recipe

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F and line a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Cream butter and sugar until pale. Beat in eggs gradually.
  3. Fold in flour, coconut, baking powder and salt with coconut milk.
  4. Bake 40–45 minutes until golden and firm in the centre.
  5. Cool completely before serving; toast a little coconut for the top if desired.

WHY IT WORKS

Watch the signals, not just the clock.

The exact minute can vary with your oven, tin and batter depth. Look for colour, spring-back, a set centre and the smell of a fully baked crumb.
